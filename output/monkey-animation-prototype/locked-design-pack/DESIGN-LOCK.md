# Approved character designs

The user approved this lineup and locked its design on 2026-09-23. `approved-design.png` is the source of truth. New animation poses must preserve it; do not replace these species with recolored common monkeys.

1. Common monkey: compact brown body, cream face/belly, round ears, curled tail.
2. Chimpanzee: charcoal body, projecting beige muzzle, long heavy arms, short legs, no tail.
3. Gorilla: bulky purple-gray body, silver chest, crest, huge forearms, no tail.
4. Orangutan: orange fur, blue-gray cheeked face, long shaggy arms, short legs, no tail.
5. Gibbon: slender buff body, black face with pale ring, black hands, very long thin limbs, no tail.

Keep the approved pixel density, clean dark outlines, restrained shading, facial features, palette and species-specific proportions. Existing roll, slap and separate grab-arm designs remain approved. Grab arms stay modular; do not bake branches or vines into them.

New animation sheets are pose studies derived from this reference. Minor generation drift is not a revision to the locked designs. Before game integration, align poses and pivots, remove backgrounds and refine in-between frames.
